How it works

Four simple steps

The same flow we analysed in our system map: Client → Server → Payment → Email → Confirmation.

  1. Choose

    Pick your dates and a room type. You see the number of free rooms before you decide.

  2. Your details

    Enter name, email and phone. Add a special request, for example a quiet room.

  3. Pay by card

    Your card is turned into a secure token in your browser. The hotel never sees the card number.

  4. Confirmation

    You get a booking number on the screen and a confirmation email in less than one minute.
